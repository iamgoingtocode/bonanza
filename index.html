Two companies, one Bonanza

One group, built around the paperwork that usually stalls people's plans.

Bonanza Group of Companies was formed to put two everyday problems under one Ugandan roof: an employer who needs verified workers fast, and a patient who needs treatment that isn't available at home yet. Both problems come down to the same bottleneck — documentation, clearance and travel — so both companies are run out of the same desk, by the same standard.

Bonanza Staffing Solutions Limited recruits, vets and deploys workers, and helps job seekers find and prepare for verified vacancies. Bonanza Medical Tours and Travel coordinates referrals, visas and logistics for patients travelling for care. Different clients, same commitment: nothing moves until the paperwork is confirmed in writing.

Bonanza Medical Tours and Travel

What's included

  • Hospital and specialist referral matched to the diagnosis
  • Written treatment estimate confirmed before travel
  • Medical visa and attendant visa documentation support
  • Airport transfer, accommodation and local travel
  • Interpreter and attendant support during the stay
  • Discharge summary handover and follow-up scheduling

Who this is for

  • Patients referred for specialist treatment not yet available locally
  • Families who need a second opinion from a partner hospital abroad
